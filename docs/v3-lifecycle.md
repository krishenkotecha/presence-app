# Presence — The Relationship Lifecycle Map

The core pain (`v3-direction.md`) is constant at every stage: the gap between what's *said*
and what's *meant*. What shifts predictably is **which layer dominates** — and therefore
**which product surface is the hero.**

---

## The stages

| Stage | Dominant feeling | Hero surface | Key risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Talking / early dating** | Uncertainty (almost no shared model) | **Decode** ambiguous signals + **prep** ("how do I bring up X?") | Most speculative read; manipulation/scripting risk — *scaffold not script* is non-negotiable |
| **New couple (0–2 yrs)** | Forming the shared model | **Prep + reflect + first repair**; couples mode + memory begin | Over-coaching a young bond; keep it light |
| **Established / cohabiting** | Recurring patterns + logistics | **Recurring-theme thread**, repair, maintenance | The memory can feel like surveillance — frame as growth |
| **Long-term / married / kids** | Drift, roommate-ification, resentment | Noticing drift, connection bids, appreciation, re-discovery | Acute decode is rare here; don't lead with it |
| **Crisis / rupture / repair** | Both layers maxed | Support understanding + repair | Must NOT play therapist; abuse/danger guard is critical |

---

## The strategic gift

The product's two halves map cleanly onto the two ends of the lifecycle:

- **Acute decode = the acquisition surface.** It wins early dating — single-player, cheap and
  fast, huge market.
- **Longitudinal memory = the retention / moat surface.** It wins the long-term stage, where
  the chronic "same fight again" pain dominates.

So land-and-expand isn't only solo→couple — it's **early→long-term**. A person can enter solo
while dating and *age into* the high-moat couples-memory product as their relationship matures.
The user's own relationship growing is the expansion path.

---

## The caveats

- **Pick a beachhead; don't span the whole lifecycle at launch.** "A relationship app for every
  stage" risks being for none — dating-decode and 20-year-marriage-maintenance have different
  users, brands, and competitors.
- **Recommended play:** use the stage-agnostic **solo decode as the acquisition wedge** (it
  works whether you're dating or married), but **anchor the durable product and brand in
  committed relationships**, where the memory compounds and trust runs deepest. Make *"we grow
  with you"* the expansion narrative, not the v1 scope.
- **Relationship-agnostic engine.** The same said→meant→need translation works for family,
  co-parents, friends, and work. Real additional TAM, but a *horizontal* that dilutes
  positioning — a later move, after winning one vertical.

---

## The link to matchmaking

The stage *before* dating is **finding someone to connect with at all**. That extends the pain
"to the left" into the authentic-connection / matchmaking thesis — same self-model asset,
pointed pre-relationship. See `v3-authentic-connection.md`.
